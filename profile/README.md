### **Web Design, Development & Digital Growth**

Fronsky is a digital studio based in Maastricht, the Netherlands. We build fast, modern websites and web applications, improve visibility across Google and AI platforms, and provide reliable hosting and technical support.

Our focus is simple: creating digital solutions that help businesses grow.

---

## 🛠️ What We Do

* **Web Design:** Modern, responsive and conversion-focused websites tailored to each business.
* **Google & AI Ranking:** SEO, GEO and AIO strategies designed to improve visibility in Google, ChatGPT and other AI-driven search platforms.
* **Web Development:** Custom web applications, platforms, integrations and digital tools built around real business requirements.
* **Hosting & Technical Infrastructure:** Fast, secure and reliable hosting with SSL, backups, monitoring and ongoing technical support.
* **Digital Products:** Developing our own web applications and SaaS products alongside client projects.
